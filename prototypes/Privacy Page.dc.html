Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Wubble is a one-person web studio from the Netherlands. This policy explains which personal data I collect through wubble.nl, why, and what your rights are. Questions? Email hello@wubble.nl.

Who is responsible

Wubble, [legal name], [address], the Netherlands. Chamber of Commerce (KvK) number [number]. I am the controller of your personal data under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

What I collect and why

  • Contact and inquiry forms: your name, email, website URL, company, the plan or package you choose and your message. Used to answer your inquiry and prepare a proposal. Legal basis: steps at your request before a contract.
  • Client work: contact and billing details and the information needed to deliver the work. Legal basis: performance of a contract and legal obligations (Dutch tax law requires keeping invoices for 7 years).
  • Chat assistant: the messages you type in the "Let's chat" window are sent to an AI service to generate an answer. Please do not share sensitive information there. Legal basis: legitimate interest in answering visitor questions.
  • Cookies and similar technology: see the cookie section below.

Cookies

  • Necessary: make the site work and remember your cookie choice. Always on.
  • Analytics: anonymous insight into how visitors use the site. Only with your consent (EU) or until you opt out (US).
  • Marketing: measure and personalise ads and campaigns. Only with your consent (EU) or until you opt out (US).

I never sell your personal data. I only use service providers that process data on my behalf under a data processing agreement:

  • Webflow (website hosting and forms)
  • Cloudflare (chat assistant backend)
  • Anthropic (AI model that answers chat messages)
  • [Email and invoicing provider]
  • [Analytics provider, only after consent]

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Transfers are protected by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How long I keep data

  • Inquiries that do not lead to a project: up to 12 months.
  • Client and invoice records: 7 years (Dutch legal requirement).
  • Your cookie choice: 12 months, then you are asked again.

Your rights (EU and the Netherlands)

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, to receive your data in a portable format, and to withdraw consent at any time. Email hello@wubble.nl and I will respond within one month. You can also file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Your rights (United States)

Residents of California and other US states with privacy laws can ask to know, correct and delete their personal information, and opt out of its sale or sharing for targeted advertising. I do not sell personal information. To opt out of sharing, use the "Do not sell or share my personal information" link in the cookie banner, or Cookie settings. I honour Global Privacy Control signals. I will not treat you differently for using these rights.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored only with providers that use appropriate security measures. Access is limited to me.
